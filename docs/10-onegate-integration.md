## 2. Commercial Separation Policy

To preserve user trust and brand integrity, the platform enforces strict separation rules:

1. **Permanent Free Independence:** HelpGate will never require an account on OneGate Core or any commercial service to use free utilities.
2. **No Aggressive Upselling:** Free tools must never display bait-and-switch dialogs (e.g. "To format more than 50 lines of JSON, purchase OneGate Enterprise").
3. **Explicit Labeling:** All commercial references are strictly housed in a designated showcase section labeled **"More from One Gateway"** in the footer and an informative About page.
4. **Independent Terms & Disclaimers:** HelpGate's privacy and terms apply exclusively to `helpgate.in`. Commercial enterprise products maintain their own contractual terms.
